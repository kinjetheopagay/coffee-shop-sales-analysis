
# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis — Project Findings


## 1. Overview

This document presents the key findings from the SQL analysis of the Coffee Shop Sales dataset, covering January to June 2023.

The analysis focuses on overall revenue, store performance, product categories, monthly and hourly sales trends, and product performance.

The objective is to identify sales patterns and understand which stores, categories, and products contribute most to the business's revenue.

## 2. Overall Sales Performance

The dataset generated a total revenue of **$698,812.33** during the six-month reporting period.

The average daily revenue was approximately **$3,860.84**.

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | $698,812.33 |
| Average Daily Revenue | $3,860.84 |
| Reporting Period | January–June 2023 |

**Key Finding:**

The coffee shop generated approximately $698.8K in revenue across the six-month period, with an average daily revenue of approximately $3.86K.

## 5. Monthly Sales Performance

The analysis examined monthly revenue from January to June 2023 to identify changes in sales performance throughout the reporting period.

| Month | Total Revenue |
|---|---:|
| January | $81,677.74 |
| February | $76,145.19 |
| March | $98,834.68 |
| April | $118,941.08 |
| May | $156,727.76 |
| June | $166,485.88 |

**Key Findings:**

- June recorded the highest monthly revenue at $166,485.88.
- February recorded the lowest monthly revenue at $76,145.19.
- Revenue declined from January to February before increasing every month from March through June.
- Revenue increased by approximately 118.6% between February and June.

**Business Insight:**

Monthly revenue showed an overall upward trend after February. June generated more than twice February's revenue, indicating stronger recorded sales performance toward the end of the reporting period.

However, the dataset covers only six months, so this pattern should not be treated as proof of a long-term seasonal trend.

## 9. Key Takeaways

The SQL analysis of the Coffee Shop Sales dataset revealed several important findings:

1. **Overall Revenue:** The coffee shop generated $698,812.33 in total revenue from January to June 2023, with an average daily revenue of approximately $3,860.84.

2. **Store Performance:** Hell's Kitchen generated the highest revenue among the three locations, although revenue was relatively close across all stores.

3. **Product Categories:** Coffee and Tea were the leading revenue-generating categories, accounting for approximately 66.7% of total revenue combined.

4. **Monthly Trends:** Revenue generally increased after February, reaching its highest point in June at $166,485.88.

5. **Hourly Performance:** The highest revenue-generating hours were between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, with 10:00 AM ranking first.

6. **Product Performance:** Barista Espresso generated the highest revenue, while Brewed Chai tea had the highest quantity sold.

7. **Revenue Contribution:** The top four product types contributed 44.49% of total revenue, highlighting their importance to overall sales performance.

### Overall Conclusion

The analysis identified differences in revenue performance across stores, product categories, months, hours, and product types.

Coffee and Tea were the strongest product categories, while a small group of product types accounted for a substantial share of total revenue. The results also showed an upward monthly revenue trend during the latter part of the reporting period.

These findings provide a foundation for understanding the coffee shop's sales performance and identifying areas for further investigation.
